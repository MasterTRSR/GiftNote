GiftNote

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 06-09-2026

GiftNote respects your privacy. GiftNote is designed to help you save gift ideas and reminders while keeping your information stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

GiftNote stores information that you manually enter into the app. This may include:

  • person names
  • relationships
  • gift ideas
  • notes
  • planned reminder dates
  • birthday or anniversary notes

How Data Is Used

The information you enter is used only to provide app functionality such as saving gift ideas, organizing ideas by person, searching memories, and showing local reminders on your device.

Data Storage

GiftNote stores your data locally on your device. GiftNote does not require an account and does not intentionally upload your personal entries to a remote server.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, GiftNote may send local reminder notifications on your device for planned gift dates.

Third-Party Services

GiftNote does not intentionally use third-party analytics, advertising, or account services to collect your personal entries. If third-party services are added in the future, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

Children’s Privacy

GiftNote is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
